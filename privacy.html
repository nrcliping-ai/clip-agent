Clip Agent – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Clip Agent is a personal desktop tool used by a single individual (the owner) with the owner's own TikTok account.

Data we access

  • user.info.basic – basic profile information of the owner's account, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • video.upload – used only to send video clips approved by the owner to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts.

Storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. No data of other TikTok users is collected.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions); local tokens are then deleted.

Changes

This policy may be updated by publishing a new version on this page.
