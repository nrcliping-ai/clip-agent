Clip Agent – Terms of Service

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Clip Agent is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool operated by a single individual for managing their own content. It prepares short video clips and, only after the owner manually approves each clip, sends it to the owner's own TikTok account as a draft (TikTok inbox). The owner reviews, edits and publishes every draft manually in the TikTok app.

Use

  • The tool is used only by its owner and only with the owner's own TikTok account.
  • No content is posted publicly by the tool; all uploads are drafts that require manual publishing.
  • The owner is responsible for ensuring that published content complies with TikTok's Community Guidelines and applicable law, including rights to the content.
